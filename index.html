THERMODYNAMICS

Why
Silicon-28

Silicon's latent heat of fusion — 1,800 kJ/kg — is among the highest of any abundant element. Its 1,414°C melt point places the phase-change plateau within reach of modern refractory materials.

Isotopically pure Silicon-28 eliminates the Soret thermophoretic drift that causes heavier isotopes (Si-29, Si-30) to migrate over decades, degrading performance and structural integrity.

Key correction: Liquid silicon is 5% denser than solid — it contracts on melting. The 300% expansion myth comes from silicon-lithium battery cycling, an entirely different process.
