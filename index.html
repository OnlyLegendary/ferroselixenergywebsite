Our Mission

Energy stored as heat — not chemistry. Not combustion. Not radiation.

Ferro-Silex Energy Systems is developing the FSC-6.3 Titan — a non-nuclear thermal energy storage platform built for the most demanding environment humans have ever attempted to inhabit: the surface of the Moon.

Where lithium batteries fail in extreme cold, where solar panels go dark for 14 days, where radioactive payloads require years of regulatory clearance — the Titan operates. Silently. Without moving parts. Without toxic chemistry. Without fear of fire.

The same platform that survives the lunar night will power the homes and highways of Earth. One technology. Three markets. Unlimited potential.

The Technology

FSC-6.3 Titan
Thermal Energy Platform

01 — Core
Yolk-Shell Silicon-28
Isotopically pure Silicon-28 micro-encapsulated in 150-micron Rhenium ALD shells, suspended in an isostatic graphite honeycomb matrix. At 2,000°C, silicon stores 1,787 kJ/kg of latent heat — energy released with perfect consistency across hundreds of charge cycles.
1,787 kJ/kg
Latent heat capacity
02 — Conversion
Rad-Hard TPV Array
Gallium Antimonide III-V semiconductor cells convert core radiation directly to electricity at >40% theoretical efficiency. Zero moving parts. Radiation-hardened for a decade of lunar surface exposure. Power output degrades less than 4% over a 10-year mission lifecycle.
>40%
Conversion efficiency
03 — Containment
10-Layer Architecture
Rhenium-HfC diffusion barrier. Ultra-high vacuum gap. 40-layer W/Rh MLI stack. Kinematic HfC standoff pin suspension. Grade-5 Titanium pressure vessel. Kevlar-Nextel Whipple shield. ITO-coated alumina exterior. A system designed to operate without maintenance for 10 years.

Design mission life
